Free online games you can play instantly

Yayzu is a home for small, fun browser games. Tap a game and you're playing: no downloads, no installs, no sign-up.

Play anywhere

Every game on Yayzu runs right in your browser, on a phone, tablet, laptop or desktop. On a phone, swipe and tap. On a computer, use the keyboard. Games load fast and work on modern versions of Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox.

Always free

All games on Yayzu are free to play. There's no account to create and nothing to install, so you can jump into a quick game whenever you have a minute.

Made by indie developers

Yayzu games are built by small, independent developers using modern web technology like three.js and WebGL. We're starting small and adding new games regularly, so check back often.

Start with Boulder Bear

Our first game, Boulder Bear, puts a brave teddy at the foot of a mountain while boulders come rolling down three hiking trails. Dodge them, jump them, or hop on top and ride them for as long as you can.
