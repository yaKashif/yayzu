Free online games you can play instantly

Yayzu is a home for small, fun browser games. Tap a game and you're playing: no downloads, no installs, no sign-up.

Play anywhere

Every game on Yayzu runs right in your browser, on a phone, tablet, laptop or desktop. On a phone, swipe and tap. On a computer, use the keyboard or mouse. Games load fast and work on modern versions of Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox.

Always free

All games on Yayzu are free to play. There's no account to create and nothing to install, so you can jump into a quick game whenever you have a minute.

Made by indie developers

Yayzu games are built by small, independent developers using modern web technology like three.js and WebGL. We're starting small and adding new games regularly, so check back often.

Games to start with

Boulder Bear puts a brave teddy at the foot of a mountain while boulders come rolling down three hiking trails. Dodge them, jump them, or hop on top and ride them for as long as you can.

Fruit Slash fills the screen with flying fruit for you to slice with a swipe. Chain combos for bonus points, and steer your blade clear of the bombs.

Wiz o Wisp is a relaxing piano game: hop a glowing wisp up steps of light, one note per hop, and play songs from Twinkle Twinkle to Beethoven on the beat.
