FJ40 and world

An open differential lets its tires turn at different speeds round a corner, but a tire on ice gets all the spin. Locked, they turn together.

Lower squashes the tires more: a bigger contact patch, more rolling resistance.

Naseeb

A 1970 Toyota Land Cruiser FJ40, built up from its tires: the ladder frame, the 3.9-litre F six, the gearbox and transfer case, both axles driven. Drive it across asphalt, grass, sand, mud, snow and ice, and watch every tire grip and slip. (Its body comes later.)
