Boulder Bear

Boulder Bear

Boulders are rolling down the mountain, and one brave teddy bear is standing right in their way. Dodge them, jump them, or hop on top and ride them. How long can you keep the bear in one piece?

About the game

Boulder Bear is a free arcade runner you play in your browser. Your teddy stands at the foot of a mountain with three hiking trails in front of it. Boulders tumble down the trails toward you, some on their own and some in long, rumbling trains of four. The boulders roll faster the longer you survive, so every run gets harder.

How to play

  • Change trails to get out of a boulder's way.
  • Jump over a single boulder.
  • Ride a train: a train of four boulders is too long to jump over, but you can jump on top and run along it until it rolls past.
  • Drop mid-jump to land sooner.
  • Your score grows the longer you survive, with 10 bonus points for every boulder that rolls past.

Tips

  • Only the front of a train can knock you over. Land anywhere along its top and you're safe.
  • When all three trails are blocked, one of them always has a single boulder you can jump.
  • Listen for the rumble: it gets louder as boulders get close.
  • Jump a little early. A late jump into the front of a boulder still counts as a hit.

Questions

Is Boulder Bear free?

Yes. It's free to play, with no account and nothing to download.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It's built for phones held upright as well as computers. Use swipes to play.

Why is there no sound?

Browsers only allow sound after you tap or press a key, so it starts when you press Play. Check the speaker button in the corner, or press M.

Is my high score saved?

Your best score is saved in your browser on this device.
