The top

Naseeb: The Jeep Driver

You are Naseeb, a jeep driver in the far north, where the roads are cut into cliff faces, the bridges are planks over glacier rivers, and a landslide can close the way overnight. You make your living taking people and supplies where no bus will go, in an old army-green jeep: a straight six, three gears in high and low range, four-wheel drive and leaf springs.

Today's job: take the jeep up the mountain. The track is a narrow ledge of dirt cut into the slope, the mountain on one side and the drop on the other. Some of the hairpins are too tight to get round in one go: stop, back up, and go again. One mistake and you're over the edge.

▲ throttle, ▼ or Space brakes, ◀ ▶ steer (or arrows / WASD). Hold ▼ to stop, then to back up. Gears 1–6 (1 is Low 1st, for the steepest bits), 0 automatic. L diff locks, V view, K sound, Backspace back onto the road.
