Fruit Slash

Fruit Slash

Fruit is flying everywhere and you've got the sharpest swipe in town. Slice it all in half, chain big combos, and whatever you do, don't touch the bombs.

About the game

Fruit Slash is a free fruit-slicing arcade game you play in your browser. Watermelons, oranges, apples, lemons, kiwis and plums are tossed up from the bottom of the screen, and you cut them out of the air with a swipe. Each one splits in two with a splash of juice. Bombs with lit fuses are thrown in among the fruit, so watch where your blade goes. The longer you last, the faster and busier it gets, until it levels off at a pace that's tough but always fair.

How to play

  • Swipe across a fruit to slice it. Each fruit is worth one point.
  • Combos: slice three or more fruit in a single swipe for bonus points, one extra point for every fruit in the combo.
  • Bombs: the black ones with the red glow. Cut one and the game is over on the spot.
  • Lives: every fruit that falls back down uncut costs a life. Miss three and the game ends.

Tips

  • Wait for fruit to bunch up near the top of its arc, then cut them all in one long swipe.
  • A slow drag won't cut anything. Swipe quickly.
  • When a bomb flies up next to a fruit, cut the fruit from the far side, away from the bomb.
  • Lift your finger between swipes so you can't drag through a bomb by accident.
  • Listen for the fizz of a lit fuse: it means there's a bomb on screen.

Questions

Is Fruit Slash free?

Yes. It's free to play, with no account and nothing to download.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works on phones and tablets, upright or sideways, as well as on computers.

Why is there no sound?

Browsers only allow sound after you tap or press a key, so it starts when you press Play. Check the speaker button in the corner, or press M.

Is my high score saved?

Your best score is saved in your browser on this device.
